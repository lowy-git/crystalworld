    DescriptionSpinel twin.png
    English: Diagram of contact twinning of spinel. The image at left is a single crystal with the composition plane outlined in red. At right are crystal twins, corresponding to cutting the single crystal on its composition plane and rotating the front half clockwise. After (1964) Mineralogy for amateurs., Princeton, N.J.: Van Nostrand, pp. 96−105 ISBN: 0442276249.
